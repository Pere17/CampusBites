# CampusBites — Test Summary

Generated by `testing/run-tests.js` against the live application (localhost:3000),
backed by a freshly seeded `data/db.json`, then post-processed by
`testing/postprocess-test-data.js` and summarized by `testing/compute-summary.js`.
Raw computed figures: `testing/summary-raw.json`.

The 2 genuine failures are real, reproducible application gaps, not test-script errors:
1. Malformed email addresses (e.g. `not-an-email`) are accepted by `POST /register` — format validation exists only client-side.
2. FR7 (vendor menu management: add/edit/remove items) is not implemented in the codebase.

Both are documented in full in Chapter 4 and in the CSVs.
